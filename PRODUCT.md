# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers who land on abhiram.cyou: peers in tech writing and software, hiring
managers, and people who followed a link to one of his essays. They arrive to
read a piece, get a sense of who Abhiram is, and maybe browse more. Low
commitment, reading-first.

## Product Purpose

A personal site for Abhiram Chakkiyar, a writer and documentation lead (not an
engineer; he builds with AI). It publishes essays
(long-form) and notes (short technical), shows a few projects, and carries a
clear About. Success is simple: a piece is pleasant to read, the person feels
real and credible, and a reader wants to read a second thing.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, quietly confident. A writer's site, not a startup's. Voice is
warm, plain, first-person, no em-dashes, no hype. Coastal-marine palette over a
warm-neutral ground. It should feel considered and human, the work of someone
who cares about clarity in docs, code, and pixels.

## Anti-references

- Generic dev-blog dark terminal theme.
- SaaS landing-page tropes: hero-metric blocks, identical icon-card grids,
  per-section uppercase eyebrows, gradient text.
- Over-decorated "designy" treatments: heavy textures, watercolor washes, serif
  display everywhere (deliberately removed earlier; simpler read better).
- Cream/sand AI-default warm near-white as the whole identity.

## Design Principles

- Words first. The reading experience is the product; chrome serves it.
- Quiet confidence. Restraint over decoration; one accent, used well.
- Honest and human. Real voice, real work, nothing fabricated or inflated.
- Coastal, not corporate. Marine identity carried by accent and type, not noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum for all text (body already ~11:1). Full light/dark support,
keyboard and screen-reader friendly (AstroPaper baseline, do not regress),
`prefers-reduced-motion` honored for any motion added.
