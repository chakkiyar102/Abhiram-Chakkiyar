A sixty-second teaser for a new AI app-builder feature, produced end to end with AI as the hands and me directing. The app itself was built from a written spec. The opening b-roll was generated and de-watermarked, the screens were captured and cut on a timeline, and the captions are translucent gradient-glass cards composited with HyperFrames and ffmpeg.

The point of the exercise: a documentation lead, not a coder, can run the whole promo pipeline as long as they know what good looks like and keep saying so until they see it.

The full nine-stage build breakdown lives in the essay linked above.
